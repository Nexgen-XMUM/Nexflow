WHY ORBYTE BUILT NEXGEN

Closing work was scattered. Good bread was getting left behind.

Small bakery teams often count surplus by hand, work out discounts separately, write promotions after the rush, then decide what to sell, box, donate or discard with information spread across notes and messages. The result is extra closing work and little clarity about what happened next.

01 · COUNT BY HAND

One more task at closing

Leftover quantities can be recorded in different places, making the final count slow to reconcile.

02 · OFFERS IN A RUSH

Pricing and promos split apart

Markdowns, cost checks and customer messages are easy to handle as separate tasks.

03 · OUTCOMES UNCLEAR

No simple record of the next step

Sale, pickup, donation and waste decisions can be hard to review together the next day.

Orbyte built Nexgen to connect that last-hour routine: count once, check a fair next step, and keep an honest record of the outcome.

THE NEXGEN TOOLKIT

Six practical parts of a clearer close

Deterministic pricing checks protect the margin; lightweight AI and templates help prepare offers for owner review.

01

End-of-day stocktake

Record leftover quantities once and organize the count against the product catalogue. The sample YOLO scan below is separate from live stock.

02

Clearance pricing & margin guard

Check markdown prices against product cost and flag below-cost offers before an owner approves them.

03

Owner-reviewed customer offers

Prepare AI-assisted or template promo copy for review. WhatsApp messages are not sent from this page.

04

Surprise box to pickup

Prepare a surplus offer, review the customer quote and checkout flow, confirm pickup and track loyalty points.

05

Food rescue & handover

Record safe surplus as a donation and confirm the handover. Recipient profiles here are placeholders.

06

Dashboard & impact visibility

Review paid sales, pickups and donations alongside an estimated CO₂e footprint. Impact estimates are not certified.
