Features
01

Count leftovers in one line.

Staff type or say 12 croissants, 5 chocolate slices before closing. NexFlow matches each item and its cost price.

02

Auto pricing with a margin floor.

The owner sets the clearance discount. Built-in checks stop any price from dropping below cost.

03

One-tap WhatsApp to regulars.

Once approved, the deal goes to the regulars group, with read ticks and reservations simulated for the demo.

04

A dashboard the owner reads at a glance.

Monthly target, recovered revenue, food saved by weight, and which categories sell best.

05

Owner admin panel.

Edit cost prices, slide the discount rate, and set the message header and group name. Changes sync both ways.

06

SDG 12.3 carbon footprint.

Every clearance logs the CO₂e and grams of food kept out of the bin, ready for the shop's ESG story.
